## 📘 Unit Overview
(This unit  focuses on the foundational principles of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and its growing impact on modern enterprise systems. Students will explore the differences between AI, Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning, gaining insights into how these technologies power intelligent systems. The week will also delve into practical applications of AI, such as automation, predictive analytics, and natural language processing, as well as address the ethical challenges associated with AI adoption.

By engaging with key AI concepts, students will gain a deeper understanding of how AI can enhance decision-making, optimise business operations, and transform industries. The discussion of ethical considerations will help students critically analyse issues like bias in AI models, transparency, and accountability.)

## 📝 Assignment for unit 06 Implementing and Evaluating a Simple AI Model

Using Python and the Scikit-learn library, implement a simple AI model for a basic prediction task (e.g., predicting housing prices, classifying email as spam or not spam). Train the model on a publicly available dataset (e.g., UCI Machine Learning Repository) and test its accuracy. Evaluate the model’s performance (e.g., accuracy, precision, recall) and discuss its limitations.

Create your Python code, the dataset used, and a 300-word analysis discussing:

The problem the AI model solves.
The results and model performance metrics.
Ethical considerations for the dataset and AI model use case (e.g., potential biases).
