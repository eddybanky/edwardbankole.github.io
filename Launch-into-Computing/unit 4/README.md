## 📘 Unit Overview
(Software engineering is a critical discipline in computing, enabling the design, development, and maintenance of reliable and efficient software systems. This unit introduces the principles and processes of software engineering, focusing on the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), software design methodologies, and the importance of sustainable software development.

This unit explore the key phases of the SDLC, including requirements gathering, design, implementation, testing, and maintenance, while comparing different development methodologies such as Waterfall, Agile, and DevOps. Discussions will also cover the role of software engineering in enterprise systems, emphasising best practices for delivering high-quality software that meets functional and non-functional requirements)

## 📝 Assignment for unit 04
Select two software development methodologies (e.g., Waterfall, Agile, or DevOps). Compare the two methodologies based on the following factors:

Flexibility and adaptability to changing requirements.
Suitability for small vs. large projects.
Risk management and quality assurance.
Write a 500-word analysis identifying the strengths and limitations of each methodology and recommend which approach would work best for specific project scenarios.
